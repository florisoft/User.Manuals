# Florisoft Picking App Manual

## What is the Picking App?

The Florisoft Picking App supports warehouse employees while collecting products for customer orders. It shows which work order must be processed, where the products are located, which quantity must be picked and which instructions apply.

Scans are checked immediately. The employee therefore sees whether the correct product and stock have been selected. Progress is updated throughout the process, and the work order can be completed after the final pick.

The available sections and actions depend on the configuration, user permissions and licences.

---

## Opening the app

1. Open the Florisoft Hub App.
2. Start the **Picking App**.
3. Sign in with your Florisoft user.
4. Select the available picking section.
5. Identify the employee when requested.

If the Picking App or a section is not visible, the required access or licence may be missing. Contact your administrator or Florisoft consultant.

---

## Picking App sections

### Order Picking

Order Picking guides the employee through complete work orders. Depending on the configuration, the app automatically offers a suitable work order or lets the employee select one.

The normal workflow consists of:

1. obtaining and checking a work order;
2. activating the assignment;
3. going to the specified picking locations;
4. checking and registering products and quantities;
5. processing shortages or an alternative location;
6. completing the work order;
7. optionally printing an address label, registering returnables or adding pictures.

- [Full Order Picking manual](Order%20Picking/Manual%20Order%20Picking%20-%20EN.md)

### Pallet Picking

Pallet Picking is intended for warehouse processes in which products are collected by pallet and location, for example with a forklift. The employee opens an assignment, visits the offered locations and registers products within the corresponding pallet workflow.

This section is visible only when Pallet Picking is available to the organisation. Its exact operation and configuration may differ per implementation.

---

## Integration with other Florisoft Apps

Order Picking can start additional actions:

- printing an address label;
- registering outbound returnable packaging;
- adding pictures to the work order;
- registering a discrepancy or stock shortage.

These actions are shown only when the corresponding integration and licence are available. The additional app does not always have to be opened separately on the device.

---

## Practical points

- Always work under your own employee identity.
- Check the product, location and quantity before every confirmation.
- Refresh the overview when an assignment or product may have been changed by a colleague.
- Register shortages and damage as discrepancies; do not confirm them as fully picked.
- Close a work order only after all lines have been processed.
- Contact the administrator when assignments, groups or printing options are missing.

---

## Support

The Picking App is configured centrally. An administrator or Florisoft consultant determines which assignments are visible, how picks are confirmed, and which printers and additional actions are available.
